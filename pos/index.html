FAQ

Puravigal POS questions

Who is Puravigal POS for?

It is being built for micro and small businesses including retail shops, restaurants, cafés, mobile shops, supermarkets and grocery stores.

Is Puravigal POS the first Puravigal product?

Yes. Puravigal POS is the first product being released under the Puravigal SaaS platform.

Will the POS experience change by business type?

The core product remains shared, while workflows and configurations can be adapted for different business verticals.

Can I start for free?

The first release includes a Standard offering designed to make it easy for small businesses to get started.
